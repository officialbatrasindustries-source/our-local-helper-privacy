1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Our Local Helper collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the mobile application.

2. Information We Collect

  • Name and email address.
  • Google account information when Google Sign-In is used.
  • Google profile photo, when available.
  • Phone number provided by helpers.
  • Profession, skills, experience, fees, availability, area, and profile description.
  • Approximate or precise device location when permission is granted.
  • Chat messages between users.
  • Ratings and reviews.
  • Authentication and app-related identifiers.
  • Advertising and device information processed by Google AdMob.
  • Purchase status for the optional ad-free feature through Google Play Billing.

3. How We Use Information

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Create and display helper profiles.
  • Help users discover nearby helpers.
  • Enable communication between customers and helpers.
  • Display ratings and reviews.
  • Maintain app security and prevent abuse.
  • Show ads to users who have not purchased the ad-free option.
  • Recognize users who purchased the one-time ad-free feature.
  • Improve app reliability and functionality.

4. Location Information

Our Local Helper may request approximate or precise device location to help customers find nearby helpers and to save a helper's service location. Location is accessed only after Android permission is granted.

5. Firebase and Google Sign-In

The app uses Google Firebase services, including Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore, for account authentication and app data storage.

If Google Sign-In is used, Google may provide basic profile information such as your name, email address, and profile image.

6. Helper Profiles, Messages, Ratings and Reviews

Helper profile information may be visible to other signed-in users who search for local service providers. This may include displayed name, profession, skills, fees, availability, area, profile photo, and other information you provide.

Messages are stored so conversation participants can view their chat history. Users may also submit ratings and reviews for helper profiles.

7. Advertising

Our Local Helper may display banner, native, and interstitial advertisements through Google AdMob.

Google AdMob may process device identifiers, advertising identifiers, approximate location, app interactions, diagnostics, and other information needed to provide, measure, personalize, secure, and improve advertising, subject to Google's policies and consent choices where required.

8. Remove Ads Forever

The app offers an optional one-time purchase called Remove Ads Forever, processed through Google Play Billing.

We do not receive your full payment card details. The app checks purchase ownership through Google Play so ads can remain disabled for eligible purchasers.

9. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers used to operate the app, including Google Firebase, Google Sign-In, Google AdMob, and Google Play services. Public helper profile information may be shown to other users of Our Local Helper.

10. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as needed to provide app features, maintain security, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, and meet legal or regulatory requirements.

When a helper profile is deleted, helper-profile information may be removed while the main login account remains active.

11. Account & Data Deletion

You may request deletion of your Our Local Helper account and associated data.

From inside the app

Go to Our Local Helper → My Account → Delete Account and follow the confirmation steps when this option is available in your app version.

Outside the app

If you cannot access the app, send an account deletion request from the email address associated with your account to:

official.batraindustries@gmail.com

Subject: Our Local Helper Account Deletion Request

Certain information may be retained where required for legal, security, fraud-prevention, dispute-resolution, or regulatory purposes.

12. Security

We use reasonable technical and platform security measures to protect information. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

13. Children's Privacy

Our Local Helper is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If we learn that information was collected improperly, we will take appropriate steps to remove it.

14. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app, services, or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page.
